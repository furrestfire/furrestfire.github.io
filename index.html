about

I'm Alex — FurrestFire online. Most of what I do is either reading, writing, or playing something I can get lost in. Stories are the through-line: I like characters with weight to them, and I like picking them apart until they hold together.

I write character-driven fiction — long profiles, half-finished scenes, the occasional short story — mostly in the SCP:RP / Sarkic corner of things. When I'm not writing, I'm in a game, or quietly rebuilding my own desktop so it runs faster and stays out of my way.

This page is a static HTML file. No scripts, no trackers, no analytics. Just text.
